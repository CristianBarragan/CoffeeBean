The important distinction: measured vs modeled

Measured: tool calls, estimated context-load tokens, latency, RPS, success/failure counts.
Modeled: dollar cost, energy and CO₂. These use explicit assumptions shown above. Energy is expressed as energy (Wh/kWh), not instantaneous power (W), because the benchmark does not measure electrical power draw.

The environmental argument is therefore intentionally conservative in wording: reducing the amount of model context can reduce the compute work that needs to be performed; the magnitude of the energy and CO₂ effect depends on the model, hardware, serving stack, utilization and electricity mix.

Methodology

The benchmark uses the same customer-exposure-review business task across both paths. The fixture contains 4 relationships, 12 contracts and 48 transactions per customer, with an exposure threshold of $48,000. Each cell is warmed up five times and then measured for 30 runs.

The benchmark is an end-to-end replay harness. Provider-reported model tokens are not available in replay mode, so the context figure is explicitly labeled as an estimate. The full raw Run 4 artifacts are published under assets/agent-benchmark/run4/.

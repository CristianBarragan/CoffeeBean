## Content principles

1. Write for developers first.
2. Use natural technical search phrases in headings and explanatory text.
3. Give each page one clear search intent.
4. Avoid keyword stuffing.
5. Prefer deep, useful pages over many thin pages.
6. Cross-link related concepts.
7. Keep examples concrete and runnable.
8. Keep benchmark claims scoped to the tested workload.
9. Maintain `sitemap.xml`, canonical URLs, descriptions, and OpenGraph metadata.
10. `llms.txt` and `llms-full.md` exist at the docs-site root — regenerate `llms-full.md` from the four source pages whenever their content changes, so it stays aligned with the public documentation.
